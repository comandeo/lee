Privacy policy

Lee accesses only its owner's Google Calendar, with the calendar.events permission, to read and create calendar events. Calendar data is processed on the owner's own server and, to answer the owner's requests, by the AI model provider Anthropic. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
